Deep Dive Brand & Visual Identity

Departmental Brand Identity System

Designed and delivered the complete visual identity for an internal operations department - one master mark extended into a full lockup and colour-treatment system, localized for two regions. Mocked up in Photoshop, finished as scalable vector artwork in Adobe Illustrator, then exported at every size teams need - down to a sub-200px compact lockup made for email signatures.

Brand Identity Adobe Illustrator Vector / SVG

Anonymized portfolio sample. The delivered marks are proprietary, so the specimen below uses neutral placeholder shapes to show the structure of the system rather than the artwork itself.

The Challenge

A department with no visual identity

The department showed up differently in every deck, document, and regional team - no agreed mark, no colour standards, no rules for how it should appear on a slide versus a sign. It read as an afterthought rather than a group.

The Approach

One mark, a system around it

Mocked up directions in Photoshop, then designed a single geometric mark and built a complete system around it: horizontal, stacked, compact, and mark-only lockups, each in a full set of colour treatments - drawn as vectors in Illustrator so nothing breaks from favicon to signage.

The Outcome

Consistent everywhere it appears

One coherent identity the department can apply anywhere - light or dark backgrounds, print or screen, US or Ireland - all pulled from a single source of truth instead of whatever file someone had on hand.

Process

How It Was Built

01

Concept & Mockups

Started from what the department actually does and who sees its work, sketched several mark directions, then mocked them up in Photoshop before committing - looking for something simple enough to read at a favicon and distinct enough to own.

02

Mark Design

Refined the chosen direction into a single geometric mark in Adobe Illustrator, built on a consistent grid with clean curves so it holds its shape at any size and reproduces cleanly in one colour.

03

Lockups & System

Extended the mark into horizontal, stacked, compact, and mark-only lockups, each with defined spacing and clear-space rules - so there is a correct version for a wide banner, a square avatar, or a tight footer.

04

Treatments & Delivery

Produced a full colour set - primary, reversed, single-colour, white knockout, and black - localized US and Ireland editions, and delivered scalable vector masters plus PNG exports at every size teams need, including a dedicated sub-200px compact lockup for email signatures.
